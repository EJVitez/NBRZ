# NBRZ — TikTok & Reels Hook Scripts

## How To Film These

- **Phone only, vertical, natural light.** Stand near a window or step outside — no ring light, no studio. The rawer it looks, the more the algorithm trusts it as organic content.
- **Film in one take whenever possible.** Mess-ups, resets, and real reactions outperform polished cuts. If the track title is "One Take," that's the whole bit — hit record and go.
- **Batch film on one day, post across the week.** Pick one 90-minute block per week, film 5–7 clips back to back, then schedule them. You never have to scramble the day you're posting.

### Show Me Your TikTok

**Why it can blow:** The title *is* the call-to-action — TikTok's algorithm rewards content that drives comments and duets, and this song's name literally tells viewers what to do before you say a word.

**Video concept:** You're sitting casually, phone in hand, maybe in a car or on a couch. The beat drops and you flip the camera outward like you're showing the audience your screen. ### BOOM BOOM

### Vertigo

**Why it can blow:** Single-word titles with physical/emotional weight index well in search, and the concept of vertigo — disorientation, intensity, being overwhelmed — maps to the relatable-chaos aesthetic that performs best with 18–30 audiences.

The slight visual disorientation mirrors the song title and makes the content feel intentional and craft-forward, not gimmicky.
