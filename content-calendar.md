# NBRZ — 30-Day Content Calendar

## The System (read this first)

- **30 minutes a day, max.** Film in 90-minute batches once a week (5–7 clips at a time), then schedule them. The daily 30 minutes is caption writing, hashtag copy-paste, and hitting Post — not filming.
- **Cross-post everything, change the caption.** Every video goes on TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts. The same clip. Change the caption slightly for each platform's tone (TikTok = casual/punchy, Reels = slightly more polished, Shorts = keyword-front-loaded). Never skip cross-posting — it's free reach.
- **Post Tuesday–Thursday between 7–10 PM local time** for highest baseline reach. Saturday afternoon (2–5 PM) is your secondary window. Monday and Sunday are rest days by default — protect your creative energy.
- **Engage for 20 minutes after every post.** Reply to every comment in the first hour. TikTok's algorithm heavily weights early comment velocity. One genuine reply can double a post's distribution in the first 3 hours.
- **Drop Week (Days 21–25) is the push.** Every other week is slow-building audience. Drop Week means posting daily, cross-posting aggressively to Twitter/X with video previews, and explicitly asking followers to share. Plan your best content for this window.
